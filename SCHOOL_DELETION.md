# Master access and permanent school deletion

Ordinary school navigation, quick actions and All tools have no Master Panel links. On the login page, tap/click the logo five times within five seconds, or use Alt+Shift+M, to reveal master sign-in. Hidden navigation is a presentation choice; the server separately requires valid credentials, platform-admin membership and a master-workspace session. A normal owner session is denied access to every platform endpoint.

**Archive** retains records and supports restoration. **Delete permanently** removes the entire selected organization, including all schools/branches under it, from the active app database.

1. Read the target school/code and deletion scope. Type its exact login code, supply a reason and confirm the first warning. This creates a five-minute, hashed, actor/session/target-bound proof. No accounts are logged out and no school records are deleted at this step.
2. In a separate dialog, type `DELETE school-code`, enter your current master password, and confirm the irreversible deletion. The server checks authorization again, validates the proof/password, and commits the tenant cascade in one transaction. Invalid, expired, replayed or other-session proofs cannot delete data.

Deletion removes every tenant-owned table's rows: users/roles, sessions, school/branch details, enrollments/guardian links, attendance, homework/results, fees/ledger/receipts, documents/file bytes, integrations/provider payloads, tenant audit history and queued work. All school role sessions become invalid immediately on commit; open live streams clear their screens at the next authorization check (normally within two seconds). Background/reconnecting tabs also validate their session before retaining the workspace. Logout clears dialogs, private cached records, CSRF and deletion proofs.

An organization containing any master administrator cannot be deleted, preventing platform self-lockout. A minimal master-owned security receipt retains the actor/time, deletion reason and hashed target ID; it is not a copy of school records. Global IP/security controls, unrelated organizations, existing backups and records already stored by external providers are separate. Deleting a tenant does not erase historical backups, delete a physical PostgreSQL/Supabase project or recall messages/payments at providers. Apply the documented backup retention policy and external-provider processes as needed. The confirmation UI states the active-database scope and backup/provider boundary.

Migration 015 adds direct organization cascade ownership to existing tenant tables and deferrable relationship checks for cycles. Tenant audit history remains append-only during normal operations; audit deletion is permitted only within a tenant cascade after the owning organization has been removed. Runtime role permissions remain restricted; run the role configurator after migrations.

Verification uses disposable fixtures only. Tests assert that every `org_id` table is empty for the deleted target, files/fees/jobs are gone, all six roles lose their sessions, another organization survives, and invalid confirmation attempts preserve the target. Browser checks inspected both confirmation dialogs on a separate synthetic preview and cancelled before the final deletion button. No real school was deleted during implementation.
